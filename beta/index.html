macOS

BidVision is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like a normal app.

  1. Unzip the downloaded file, then drag BidVision into your Applications folder.
  2. Double-click BidVision. The first time, macOS may ask whether you're sure you want to open an app downloaded from the Internet. Click Open. That's it.

If macOS ever says it “can't be opened”: open the Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password. (On macOS Sequoia the old right-click → Open trick no longer works, so this is the path now.)

Windows

  1. Run the downloaded installer (.exe).
  2. If SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC,” click More info, then Run anyway. This appears because the app is new. It's not a virus warning.
  3. Follow the installer. BidVision opens when it finishes, and lives in your Start Menu from then on.

No Run anyway button? Right-click the .exe → Properties → check Unblock → OK, then run it again.

Android

  1. Tap Android phone & tablet above to download the .apk file.
  2. Open the downloaded file, from the download notification or your Files / Downloads app.
  3. Android will ask permission to install from this source. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back. (On Android 15 this is granted per app: allow it for the app you downloaded with, usually your browser or Files app.)
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. To update later, download the new .apk and install it over the top.

What BidVision does

Your bid sheet has over a thousand sequences. BidVision searches them and finds the ones that fit your schedule and your preferences.

Your schedule, built around your life

Mark the days that matter (a wedding, a trip, your kid's recital) and BidVision finds trips that work around them. Or start from the trips you like and see which days they fall on. It works either way.

Your preferences, in your order

Every filter is a preference you can weight, not just a checkbox. Drag them into a priority order and BidVision breaks ties the way you would: your must-haves move a trip further than your nice-to-haves.

Soft filters find hidden gems

A hard yes-or-no filter hides everything else. BidVision lets you prefer, allow, or exclude, so trips that almost match still show up, scored and ranked. You might find a sequence you'd never have seen otherwise.
