BidVision

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026 · Applies to the BidVision private beta (April to October 2026) and to the notify list that continues after it

BidVision is a bid-sheet search tool for airline crew. It read your monthly bid package and helped you search and filter it. The private beta is over and nothing is available to download. This policy explains, plainly, what the app did with your information and what I still hold, which comes almost entirely from the signup form.

BidVision is made by FoxFinch Adaptations LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company. In this policy, "I," "me," and "my" mean FoxFinch Adaptations LLC.

This policy covers the BidVision beta and the notify list that outlives it. The beta was free and required no account. This policy does not promise or describe any future product. If I change how I handle your information, I will update this page (see section 10).

The short version

  • Your bid sheets stay on your device. Bid sheets, availability and preferences were read on your device and never uploaded.
  • The app needed no account and no sign-in to do its job.
  • I never sell, rent, or advertise with your data.
  • What is stored elsewhere: the name, email and base you typed into the signup form. For earlier signups it also includes your platform, and your phone number and group-chat preference if you gave them. I also logged each download against your email address.
  • I am keeping those details after the beta ended, so I can write to you if there is ever something new. You can ask me to delete them at any time, and I will.
  • The website runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

1.The BidVision app

When you loaded a bid package into BidVision, the app parsed and displayed it entirely on your device. Your schedule, your trip data, and the PDF itself:

  • were processed locally, on your device;
  • were not transmitted to me, to any server of mine, or to any third party;
  • stay on your device until you remove them (if you still have the app installed, its "clear all data" does this).

BidVision needed no account and no internet connection to read and filter your bid sheet. I operate no servers that receive your airline or schedule data, because there are none.

If you chose to send me a screenshot, a bid sheet, or other feedback, I received what you sent and used it only to help you and improve BidVision. It sits wherever you sent it (for example, in my email). Ask me and I will delete what I can.

2.The signup form and the notify list

The signup form was how people joined the beta. It now works only as a notify list. The form currently collects:

  • Name (a first name is fine) and email address, required;
  • Base.

Earlier signups gave more. Earlier versions of the form also collected your device/platform, and, optionally, your phone number and your group-chat/SMS preference (if you wanted to join the tester group chat). The form no longer asks for any of these, but the records from earlier signups still contain them.

Download log. While downloads were open, each download was logged against the tester's email address, with the time, the platform, and the build version, in the same spreadsheet as the signups. Nothing is downloadable now, so nothing new is being added to that log.

What I used it for. While the beta ran, I used this information to send your access code and welcome email, to send beta updates, to understand who was testing on what, and to respond to feedback. Access codes are no longer issued, so those uses have ended. I now use it only to write to you if there is ever something new worth trying. I do not use it for advertising, and I do not sell it or share it with anyone else.

Where it is stored. The signup records and the download log are in a Google Sheet that I own, in Google Workspace. Emails are sent through Google's services (Gmail and Apps Script) from hello@bidvision.app. You can ask me to update or delete your information at any time (see sections 6 and 7).

3.The website

The website (bidvision.app) is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. I do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies.

Two ordinary things happen when any website loads:

  • Hosting logs. GitHub, which serves the site, may automatically log standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to operate its servers. This is governed by GitHub's privacy practices.
  • Fonts. The pages use a web font delivered by Google Fonts. To deliver the font, Google receives your IP address. Google's use of it is governed by Google's own policy.

The signup page also stores some information in your browser's localStorage: the name and email you entered, and a few flags the page uses to remember where you were in the process. This stays in your browser on your device. Clearing your browser's site data for bidvision.app removes it.

4.Mobile beta (iPhone, iPad, Android)

  • iPhone / iPad: the iOS beta was delivered through Apple's TestFlight, using a public join link on this website. I did not send TestFlight invitations to Apple ID email addresses, and I did not collect your Apple ID. When you used TestFlight, Apple collected information (such as your email, device details, and app crash and usage data) under Apple's privacy policy, and that information sits with Apple, not with me. Because the join link was public rather than an invitation, Apple does not tell me who joined: testers appear to me only as anonymous entries with no name or email attached. Crash reports and any feedback you chose to submit through TestFlight do reach me, without your identity attached. The iOS beta build expires on or about November 11, 2026.
  • Android: the Android app was a file you downloaded and installed yourself. It was not distributed through the Google Play Store. The download was served from GitHub (see hosting logs above), and it is no longer available.

5.Service providers

I keep the toolset small. The companies that process limited information on my behalf, or in serving the site, are:

  • Google: Workspace, Sheets, Gmail and Apps Script for signup storage, the download log, and email; Google Fonts for website font delivery;
  • GitHub: website hosting, and it hosted the beta downloads;
  • Apple: iOS beta distribution via TestFlight. The beta is over, but information collected through TestFlight remains with Apple under its own policy.

Each handles data under its own privacy policy. I do not share your information with anyone else, and I never sell it.

6.Data retention

The beta has ended, and I am keeping your contact details. I am keeping the signup records (name, email, base, and for earlier signups the other fields listed in section 2) and the download log, so that I can write to you if there is ever something new worth trying. I am not promising that there will be. I have not set an end date for keeping them.

This is a change. An earlier version of this policy said I would delete your contact details when the beta ended. I am not doing that, and you may have signed up under the earlier wording, so I want to be plain about it.

You can ask me to delete your information at any time. Reply to any email from me, or write to hello@bidvision.app, and I will delete your details from the signup spreadsheet, including any phone number and your entries in the download log. Emails we have exchanged sit in my mailbox. If you want those deleted too, say so and I will.

7.Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct, or delete: email hello@bidvision.app and I will take care of it. Wherever you live, I will honor reasonable requests about the information I hold.
  • Stop hearing from me: ask to be removed, or reply to any email from me.
  • California residents (CCPA/CPRA): I do not sell or "share" your personal information, and I do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. You may request access to or deletion of the information I hold. Just email me.
  • Avoiding the web font: because the font is delivered by Google, blocking third-party requests (or using a privacy browser or extension) stops the font from loading while leaving the site fully usable.

8.Security

I use reasonable measures to protect the limited information I hold (for example, access-controlled Google Workspace). No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security. Your bid sheets never reached me in the first place, because the app kept them on your own device.

9.Children

BidVision is a professional tool for working flight attendants and is not directed to children. I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

10.Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle information, I will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. For material changes affecting people on the notify list, I will also tell you by email.
