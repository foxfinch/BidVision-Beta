BidVision

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Applies to the BidVision app (including version 0.2.0-b10), this website and the BidVision mailing list

BidVision is a bid-sheet search tool for airline crew. It reads your monthly bid sheet on your device and finds the trips that fit your schedule and preferences. A private beta ran from April to October 2026; version 0.2.0-b10 is now available to download after signing up. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does with your information and what I hold, which comes almost entirely from the signup form.

BidVision is made by FoxFinch Adaptations LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company. In this policy, "I," "me," and "my" mean FoxFinch Adaptations LLC.

The short version

  • Your bid sheets stay on your device. Bid sheets, availability and preferences are read on your device and never uploaded.
  • The app needs no account, no sign-in and no internet connection to do its job.
  • Signing up puts you on a mailing list. I store your name, email, base, and the device the signup page detected, and send occasional emails about BidVision. Unsubscribe any time.
  • Downloads are logged against your email, with the time, device and version.
  • I never sell or rent your information, and never give your email address to advertising platforms.
  • The website runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

1.The BidVision app

When you load a bid sheet into BidVision, the app parses and displays it entirely on your device. Your schedule, your trip data, your availability, your preferences and the PDF itself:

  • are processed locally, on your device;
  • are not transmitted to me, to any server of mine, or to any third party;
  • stay on your device until you remove them (the app's "clear all data" does this, as does uninstalling it).

BidVision needs no account, no sign-in and no internet connection to read and filter your bid sheet. You get your bid sheet yourself, from your airline's own site, and load the file. I operate no servers that receive your airline or schedule data, because there are none.

Android backup. Version 0.2.0-b10 turns off Android's automatic app backup, so your bid data is not copied into your Google account's device backup. Earlier Android versions allowed it, which meant Android itself could include the app's data in your own Google backup (never in anything of mine).

If you choose to send me a screenshot, a bid sheet, or other feedback, I receive what you sent and use it only to help you and improve BidVision. It sits wherever you sent it (for example, in my email). Ask me and I will delete what I can.

2.Signing up and the mailing list

You sign up to download BidVision. Signing up opens the downloads straight away. The form collects:

  • Name (a first name is fine), email address and base, all required.

The page also detects your device type from your browser (for example "Windows" or "macOS (Apple Silicon)") so it can point you at the right download, and stores it with your signup. Nothing else about your device is collected.

Earlier signups gave more. Beta-era versions of the form also asked for your platform and, optionally, your phone number and your group-chat/SMS preference. The form no longer asks for these, but records from those signups still contain them.

Download log. Each time you click a download button, the page logs it against your email address, with the time, the device and the version, in the same spreadsheet as the signups. No IP address or other device details are recorded. I use it to know which versions are installed and to tell the right people about a fix.

What I use it for. Signing up puts you on the BidVision mailing list. I send a welcome email with the download link, and after that occasional emails about BidVision: updates, fixes, new versions and other BidVision news. Every email says how to unsubscribe. I also use your details to answer feedback you send. I do not sell your information or share it with anyone other than the service providers in section 5.

Where it is stored. The signup records and the download log are in a Google Sheet that I own, in Google Workspace. Emails are sent through Google's services (Gmail and Apps Script) from hello@bidvision.app.

3.The website

The app has no analytics, tracking or third-party advertising. If that ever changes, I'll update this page before the version that changes it is available, so you can decide before you install it.

The website (bidvision.app) is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no advertising trackers or tracking cookies. Everything it records is listed on this page: your signup details, a log of downloads, and the standard data GitHub and Google Fonts receive to serve the pages. If I ever add anything, such as visitor statistics, I'll list it here before it goes live.

Two ordinary things happen when any website loads:

  • Hosting logs. GitHub, which serves the site and the downloads, may automatically log standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to operate its servers. This is governed by GitHub's privacy practices.
  • Fonts. The pages use a web font delivered by Google Fonts. To deliver the font, Google receives your IP address. Google's use of it is governed by Google's own policy.

The signup page also stores some information in your browser's localStorage: the name and email you entered, and a flag that you've signed up, so the downloads stay open when you come back. This stays in your browser on your device. Clearing your browser's site data for bidvision.app removes it.

4.Mobile (iPhone, iPad, Android)

  • Android: the Android app is a file you download from GitHub and install yourself. It is not distributed through the Google Play Store.
  • iPhone / iPad: there is no iPhone or iPad version available right now. During the beta, the iOS version was delivered through Apple's TestFlight using a public join link. I did not collect your Apple ID. Information Apple collected through TestFlight (such as your email, device details, and crash and usage data) sits with Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Because the link was public, Apple does not tell me who joined.

5.Service providers

I keep the toolset small. The companies that process limited information on my behalf, or in serving the site, are:

  • Google: Workspace, Sheets, Gmail and Apps Script for signup storage, the download log, and email; Google Fonts for website font delivery;
  • GitHub: website hosting and the app downloads;
  • Apple: iOS beta distribution via TestFlight during the beta. Information collected through TestFlight remains with Apple under its own policy.

Each handles data under its own privacy policy. I do not share your information with anyone else, and I never sell it.

6.Data retention

While you're on the list, I keep your details (the signup record and your download log entries), so I can send you BidVision emails and know which version you have.

If you unsubscribe, I stop emailing you promptly, and within 10 business days at most. I keep only your email address on a do-not-email list so I don't add you back by mistake, and delete the rest of your signup record and your download log entries.

You can ask me to delete your information at any time. Reply to any email from me, or write to hello@bidvision.app, and I will delete your details from the signup spreadsheet, including any phone number and your entries in the download log. Emails we have exchanged sit in my mailbox. If you want those deleted too, say so and I will.

If you signed up before November 9, 2026, you joined a list described as "I'll write if there's ever something new worth trying." I'll treat you the same way as everyone else on the list, and you can unsubscribe or ask for deletion at any time.

7.Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe: reply "unsubscribe" to any BidVision email, or write to hello@bidvision.app.
  • Access, correct, or delete: email hello@bidvision.app and I will take care of it. Wherever you live, I will honor reasonable requests about the information I hold.
  • California residents (CCPA/CPRA): I do not sell or "share" your personal information, and I do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. You may request access to or deletion of the information I hold. Just email me.
  • Avoiding the web font: because the font is delivered by Google, blocking third-party requests (or using a privacy browser or extension) stops the font from loading while leaving the site fully usable.

8.Security

I use reasonable measures to protect the limited information I hold (for example, access-controlled Google Workspace). No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security. Your bid sheets never reach me in the first place, because the app keeps them on your own device.

9.Children

BidVision is a professional tool for working flight attendants and is not directed to children. I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

10.Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle information, I will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. For material changes, I will also tell people on the mailing list by email.
